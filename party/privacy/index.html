Privacy Policy

This Policy explains what Party! currently sends to Discord, what GryphonEDM does not collect, and the limited information that may be received when you contact the developer.

Effective: August 3, 2026 Last updated: August 3, 2026 Operator: GryphonEDM
Current practice in plain English: Party! does not currently require an account and does not automatically send personal information or gameplay analytics to GryphonEDM. Its optional Discord Rich Presence feature sends general activity text to the Discord software already running on your device; GryphonEDM does not receive your Discord identity or account data through that feature.

1. Scope and operator

This Privacy Policy applies to Party!, related test builds, and the Party! legal pages hosted at GryphonEDM.com (collectively, the “Services”). Party! is currently developed and operated by GryphonEDM, an independent developer in California, United States.

“Proud Games” is a planned future game-company name and is not currently a separate legal entity. If a future Proud Games entity becomes the operator, this Policy will be updated to identify it.

2. Information Party! does not currently collect

As of the effective date, the current Party! build covered by this Policy does not automatically collect or transmit the following information to GryphonEDM:

  • your name, postal address, telephone number, precise location, contacts, or payment information;
  • a Party! user account, password, profile, or persistent player identifier;
  • Discord usernames, user IDs, avatars, friends, messages, voice communications, access tokens, or OAuth credentials;
  • advertising identifiers or information used for targeted advertising;
  • gameplay analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or diagnostic logs sent to a GryphonEDM-operated server; or
  • biometric, health, financial, or other sensitive personal information.

Party! is still in development. This Policy will be updated before a released feature begins collecting materially different information.

3. Discord Rich Presence

Party! includes an optional direct Discord Rich Presence integration. When the feature is enabled and the Discord desktop application is running, Party! may send Discord:

  • the Party! Discord Application ID;
  • a general activity label, such as “Main Menu” or a game-mode display name; and
  • a general gameplay description, such as “Solo,” “Local Party · 2 players,” or “4-player match.”

This information is sent to Discord through Discord’s software on your device so that Discord can display your activity according to your Discord settings. Party! does not currently use Discord OAuth, account linking, friends, messages, voice, lobbies, Join, Spectate, or account-authentication features. GryphonEDM does not receive or store your Discord account identity through Rich Presence.

Discord independently processes information under its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. You can control game-activity visibility through Discord’s settings or by closing Discord.

4. Local game data and logs

Party! may create settings, saved data, cached files, or Unreal Engine log files locally on your device. These files are not automatically transmitted to GryphonEDM under the current implementation. You control local files through your device, game installation, and operating-system tools.

If you voluntarily send a log, screenshot, video, save file, or bug report to GryphonEDM, it may contain device information, usernames visible in the material, file paths, or other information you choose to share. Review files before sending them and remove information you do not want included.

5. Information you provide voluntarily

GryphonEDM may receive information when you voluntarily send email, submit feedback, participate in testing, report a bug, or otherwise contact the developer. This can include your email address, display name, message content, attachments, technical details, and any other information you choose to provide.

This information is used to respond to you, investigate problems, improve Party!, maintain security, manage testing, enforce the Terms of Service, and comply with legal obligations.

6. Party! legal website

The Party! legal pages do not include first-party analytics, advertising trackers, account login, or forms, and they do not intentionally set first-party cookies. They are hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard connection and request information, such as IP address, browser details, and requested page, as part of providing and securing its hosting service. GitHub’s processing is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

7. How information may be shared

GryphonEDM does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information voluntarily provided to GryphonEDM may be shared only as reasonably necessary:

  • with service providers that help operate communications, development, hosting, security, or support;
  • to investigate abuse, security incidents, fraud, or violations of the Terms;
  • when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request;
  • to protect the rights, safety, and property of users, GryphonEDM, or others; or
  • as part of a future transfer of Party! to a Proud Games entity or another successor, subject to applicable law and notice through an updated Policy.

8. Retention

Because the current Game does not automatically transmit personal information to GryphonEDM, GryphonEDM does not maintain a server-side Party! account or gameplay-data retention period. Voluntary communications and support materials are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, improve and secure the Game, preserve necessary development records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations, and are then deleted or de-identified when practical.

Discord and GitHub determine their own retention practices for information they process.

9. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information voluntarily provided to GryphonEDM. No method of transmission, storage, or software operation is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Children’s privacy

Party! is intended for a broad audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The optional Discord feature is subject to Discord’s own minimum-age and account-eligibility rules. Children should not send personal information, logs, or support messages without permission from a parent or legal guardian.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has voluntarily provided personal information to GryphonEDM, contact the address below so the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the handling of personal information GryphonEDM holds about you. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Because Party! currently has no operator-held player account or persistent identifier, GryphonEDM may be unable to locate information unless you identify the communication or material you previously provided. Reasonable verification may be requested before fulfilling a privacy request.

GryphonEDM does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising. You will not be discriminated against for exercising an applicable privacy right.

12. International users

GryphonEDM operates from the United States. If you voluntarily send information from another country, it may be processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those where you live. Where required, processing is based on responding to your request, legitimate interests in developing and securing Party!, consent, performance of an agreement, or compliance with law.

13. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may change as Party! develops. The “Last updated” date will identify the latest version. Material changes may also be communicated through the Game, its distribution page, or another reasonable channel before they take effect where required by law.
